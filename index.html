Your range comes from the Vocal range tab; if you have not measured it, pick a voice type. The piano gives each key with a chord, then plays the notes with you. Any octave counts. Use headphones, or turn the piano guide off, so the microphone hears only your voice.

Press Start. Sing down to your lowest comfortable note and hold it for half a second. Then sing up to your highest comfortable note and hold it. Do not strain. Press Save when you are done. The Sing, Voice workout and Ear training tabs use the saved range.

Overlap with reference voice ranges

Voice categories are rough guides from choir singing. They are not tied to gender or age, and many voices fit more than one. Your comfortable range can grow with practice.

All sound processing happens in your browser. The app does not record or send audio, and there is no server: settings and scores stay in this browser. The app turns off the browser's echo cancellation, noise suppression and auto gain, because they distort the sound. Detection is heuristic: some voicings, very soft notes or strong overtones can give a wrong result.
