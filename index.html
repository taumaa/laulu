Voice categories are rough guides from choir singing. They are not tied to gender or age, and many voices fit more than one. Your comfortable range can grow with practice.

All sound processing happens in your browser. The app does not record or send audio. The app turns off the browser's echo cancellation, noise suppression and auto gain, because they distort the sound. Detection is heuristic: some voicings, very soft notes or strong overtones can give a wrong result.
